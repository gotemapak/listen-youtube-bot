How it works

  1. Open the botTap the button — the bot opens in Telegram on your phone or computer.
  2. Send a linkA YouTube video, Short or a whole playlist — just paste the link.
  3. ListenIn about a minute you get audio in Telegram's player plus a “📄 Video text” button.

What the bot does

  • YouTube to audio. The video's soundtrack arrives as an MP3 in Telegram's player — listen without the video, on headphones or in the car.
  • Background play, screen off. Telegram keeps playing when you switch apps or lock your phone. No YouTube Premium needed.
  • Video transcript. The “📄 Video text” button sends the transcript as a file — ask an AI to summarize it or find a specific moment.
  • Long videos and playlists. Videos up to ~11 hours arrive in parts; playlists arrive in full, track by track.
  • Yours to keep. Audio stays in the chat: listen offline, forward it to a friend or to Saved Messages.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes — you get free links every month. An unlimited plan is available via the /pay command in the bot. Each friend you invite gives you +5 bonus links.

How do I listen to YouTube with the screen off?

Send the link to the bot and play the audio it returns in Telegram. Telegram's player keeps going in the background and on the lock screen, on both Android and iPhone.

Do I need to install anything?

No. All you need is Telegram on your phone or computer — the bot runs inside it.

Which links work?

Regular YouTube videos, Shorts and playlists: youtube.com/watch, youtu.be and m.youtube.com links.

Is there a length limit?

Videos up to about 11 hours. Long recordings arrive in several parts — that's how Telegram accepts them.

Where does the transcript come from?

From YouTube's subtitles: the author's own, or the automatic captions if there are none. If a video has no subtitles at all, the bot will tell you.
