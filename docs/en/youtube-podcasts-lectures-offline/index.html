How it works

  1. Open the botTap the button — the bot opens in Telegram on your phone or computer.
  2. Send a linkA YouTube video, Short or a whole playlist — just paste the link.
  3. ListenIn about a minute you get audio in Telegram's player plus a “📄 Video text” button.

Why listen in Telegram

  • No screen needed. Audio plays in the background and on a locked phone — on a walk, while driving, at the gym.
  • Offline. Files stay in the chat — listen on the road without internet.
  • Whole playlists. A lecture course or a podcast season with one link, track by track.
  • Long recordings. Audiobooks and multi-hour streams up to ~11 hours arrive in parts.
  • Text alongside. The lecture transcript — for notes or questions to an AI.

What people listen to

University and popular-science lectures, podcasts and interviews, audiobooks and stories, book summaries, meditations and “10 hours of rain” videos for sleep and focus.

FAQ

Can I send a whole playlist?

Yes. Send the playlist link and the bot will deliver every track in order.

Is the audio saved?

Yes, it stays in your chat with the bot. Forward it to Saved Messages or a friend.

Does it work for multi-hour audiobooks?

Yes, videos up to ~11 hours are supported. A long book arrives in several parts.
